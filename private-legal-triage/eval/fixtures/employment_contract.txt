EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (SYNTHETIC EVALUATION FIXTURE)

This Agreement is made on 2 January 2026 between Brightwave Services Pvt. Ltd. ("Company") and Nisha Verma ("Employee").

1. POSITION AND PAY
The Employee will work as a Junior Analyst. The Employee shall receive a gross monthly salary of Rs. 60,000, payable on the last working day of each month.

2. PROBATION
The first six months of employment form a probation period. During probation either party may end the employment by giving 7 days' written notice.

3. TERMINATION
After probation, the Employee may resign by giving 45 days' written notice. The Company may terminate the Employee for gross misconduct without notice.

4. FAILURE TO SERVE NOTICE
If the Employee leaves without serving the full notice period, the Company may deduct an amount equal to 15 days' salary from the final settlement.5. BONUS
The Employee is eligible for a discretionary annual bonus of up to 10% of annual salary, payable at the Company's discretion.

6. LEAVE
The Employee is entitled to 18 days of paid leave per year.

7. NON-COMPETE
For 12 months after leaving the Company, the Employee shall not work for a direct competitor within the city of Sampletown.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Employee shall keep all client information confidential during and after employment.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement is governed by the laws of India.
