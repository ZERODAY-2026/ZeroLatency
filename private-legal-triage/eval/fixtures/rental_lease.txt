RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (SYNTHETIC EVALUATION FIXTURE)

This Lease Agreement is made on 15 March 2026 between Meera Iyer ("Landlord") and Karan Shah ("Tenant") for the flat at 7 Lakeview Road, Exampleville.

1. TERM
The lease term is twelve months starting 1 April 2026. The lease ends on 31 March 2027 unless renewed in writing by both parties.

2. RENT
The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of Rs. 18,500 on or before the 7th day of each month by bank transfer. If rent is paid after the 7th day, a late fee of Rs. 500 applies for each week of delay.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant has paid a security deposit of Rs. 37,000. The Landlord shall return the deposit within 30 days after the Tenant vacates, after deducting unpaid rent and the cost of damage beyond normal wear and tear.4. UTILITIES AND MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall pay electricity, water and internet charges directly to the providers. The Landlord is responsible for structural repairs. The Tenant shall arrange minor repairs costing up to Rs. 1,500 at the Tenant's own expense.

5. ENTRY BY LANDLORD
The Landlord may enter the premises for inspection on giving reasonable notice. In an emergency the Landlord may enter at any time.

6. EARLY TERMINATION
The Tenant may terminate this lease before the end of the term by giving 60 days' written notice. If the Tenant leaves without giving the full notice, the Tenant forfeits the security deposit.

7. SUBLETTING
The Tenant shall not sublet any part of the premises or assign this lease without the Landlord's written consent.

8. PETS
No pets are allowed on the premises.9. TERMINATION BY LANDLORD
The Landlord may terminate this lease on 15 days' written notice if the Tenant fails to pay rent for two consecutive months.

10. ADDITIONAL TERMS
