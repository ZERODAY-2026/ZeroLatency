Standard Residential Lease Agreement

Section 3: Term & Renewal Provisions

3.1 TERM & AUTOMATIC EXTENSION. The initial term of this Lease shall commence on the first day of September and continue through the thirty-first day of July following.

“Unless either party gives written notice at least thirty (30) days prior to the expiration of the Lease Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for an additional term of eleven (11) months under the same provisions.”

3.2 SURRENDER NOTICE. Any notice given pursuant to this Section must be delivered via registered postal delivery or certified electronic mail with acknowledged receipt to the Landlord's primary residence of record.

In the event of failure to furnish timely notice under Section 3.1, Tenant shall remain bound to pay the established monthly rental installment throughout the renewed period without abatement.
